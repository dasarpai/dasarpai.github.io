Three color planes and a sphere

X = Red · Y = Blue · Z = Green

Drag with a mouse to rotate, or use the sliders. Solid = positive direction; dashed = negative direction.

What am I seeing?

Each colored disc is the part of an infinite coordinate plane inside the sphere. Its circular rim lies on the sphere. All three planes meet at the center, and each pair shares an axis.

The colors label the geometry: red + blue light gives magenta, blue + green gives cyan, and green + red gives yellow. Transparent overlaps here are illustrative, rather than a physical light-mixing simulation.

On a Bloch sphere these are coordinate planes for one qubit. They are not paths traveled by a photon, and the colors are not physical qubit properties.
